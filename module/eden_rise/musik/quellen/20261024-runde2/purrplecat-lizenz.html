    Yes, with credit. You can use the music on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Twitch, Facebook, X and every other social platform, in videos, Shorts, Reels, podcasts, games and livestreams.

    Will I get a Content ID claim?

    No. The music on this site is not registered with Content ID, so your videos, streams and VODs won’t get claims from it.

    No. The music must never be uploaded to music streaming services such as Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, Deezer or Tidal. That includes remixes, samples and covers of it, because those uploads cause false Content ID claims for everyone else who uses the music.
