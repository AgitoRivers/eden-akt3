Enemies and threat

How fighting works in Eden Rise

One-on-one, turn by turn. There is no time pressure, except when you parry. The enemy always shows what it is about to do, and it sticks to it.

Two ways to win

Knock their life down to zero, or talk their composure down to zero. People you can talk to often have plenty of life but little composure.

The enemy announces

You always see the next intent in a speech bubble, including how much it hurts. Plan, decide, then parry.

Build, don't gamble

Levels, four paths, 16 special attacks, weapons with an element and a type. Multi-hit attacks need a shaken or exhausted enemy, so set them up.

Stats and levels

Duc grows from level 1 to 10. Enemies scale with their own level.

36 + 5

Life

36 plus 5 per level (81 at level 10). Life carries over between fights within a phase, and every new phase fills it up again.

6 / 7 / 8

Stamina

6, from level 5 it's 7, from level 9 it's 8. Always full at the start of a fight.

2

Protection

Your base, plus your jacket. Every hit you take is reduced by it.

6 + Strength

Attack

Plus your weapon's damage.

  • Experience: a win gives 20 × enemy level × threat (grey 0.5 · green 1 · orange 1.5 · skull 2).
  • Next level: 60 × your current level.
  • Skill points: one each at levels 3, 5, 7 and 9, and two at level 10. Six in total.
  • Defeat: you keep at least 12 life.

For every level an enemy is above you, it gets +25 % life and damage.

The enemy card shows composure, the threat and six attribute fields. They stay at “?” until you have tested them, and a weakness glows gold.

Basic skills

Always available, no skill points needed.

SkillAttributeStaminaEffect
TalkCharisma2d20 test: composure −6 (critical −9). Humans only.
IntimidateCourage2d20 test: the enemy's next two attacks are weakened (−3 each) and they lose 3 composure (critical 6). Works on most animals too; a boar just lowers its head.

Weakness and immunity

  • Weakness: an attribute. Tests with it roll with advantage, and a success also costs the enemy 4 composure and leaves them exhausted.
  • Immune: the skill that uses this attribute is locked, and the game tells you why (animals don't understand you, for example).
  • Fumble: you're exposed. The stamina is spent even on a failure.

Parrying

After your turn the enemy's blow is announced, and a short reaction test follows. Parrying is never a dice roll.

ResultEffect
perfectNo damage, a counter with your attack, the enemy is exhausted, +1 stamina. Three perfect parries in a row: composure −4.
good / dodgedDamage halved (at least 1).
missedFull damage.

Read them

Watch the wind-up. After a random pause a golden spark flashes, and the blow lands 450 ms later.

Scale

A circle runs along a bar. Press when it is in the golden core.

A ring closes in. D dodges, Space parries.

  • A strong special attack makes the window ×0.8. Defending first makes it ×1.5.
  • Perception widens it by 3 % per point. The Marksman path adds 10 %, the Berserker path takes 10 % away, and so does heavy armour.
  • Some blows can only be dodged, and the intent says so. The options let you set a larger parry window.

Fleeing and endings

Fleeing is a Courage test against 10 (nuisance), 14 (dangerous) or 18 (deadly). From a nightmare there's no escape, and some fights don't let you run at all. A failure costs you the round.

A fight ends in one of five ways: victory, peaceful victory (they give up), they got away, you fled or defeat. Each fight has its own consequences for every ending.

Enemies who want to run say so in their intent. If you don't finish them that turn, they're gone.

Four paths, free to choose, one step at a time.

  • Step by step: you only get a tier if you have the one below it on the same path. A finisher needs the 7 of its path.
  • Six points take one path to its finisher plus two points elsewhere, two paths to their 7, or a wider mix.
  • Main path: the path with the most points. It decides which attribute grows with your level and which trait applies. The skill screen shows what learning a skill would change.
  • Perception 4: the Marksman skills only work fully from Perception 4.
Rule of thumb: the 5s answer an announced special attack, the 9s are finishers. The exception is Retour de Flamme, which you charge beforehand.

Rosi's workshop

Once Rosi's workshop is repaired, she upgrades one piece a night: +1 damage or +1 protection, twice per piece. Hand it to her from your cabin; it costs no action, and she texts you the next morning. Each upgrade costs money and material: plates for jackets, and for weapons the next stone of their element.

Chips: collars and agent gear carry chips. Rosi cuts out the transmitter and rebuilds them so they only help you. Fit them into the slots of your weapon and jacket in the upgrade window. Before the workshop is open you can't tell what a chip does.

Packs and gangs

Up to three enemies can come at you at once. Pick your command, then your target: ← → or 1–3, Enter to strike, Esc to go back. Mistral, Lame de Fond and every grenade hit all of them. A pack rat goes down in one blow and bites softer than its alpha. Each of them swings in turn, so each blow gets its own parry.
