# Bildprompt · Test 01

Use case: stylized-concept. One production-quality painted game-icon contact sheet for Eden Rise. Intended final bitmap size 64x64 per icon. Eight separate centered icons in a regular 4-column by 2-row grid on a wide 2:1 image, genuinely transparent background, no text, labels or dividers. Large crisp category emblems, straight-on orthographic flat engineered hardware casings, bronze common-tier, one active solid indicator and two subdued solid grey pips. Richly hand-painted satin metal and broad readable highlights, warm copper repair accents, softly colored charcoal inner face, premium friendly anime-tech, no excess microdetail. Top row: red hammer/Force diamond, yellow crosshair/Precision diamond, teal solid-center cog/Technique diamond, blue return-arrow/Counter diamond. Bottom row: purple flame/Status diamond, orange single energy-coil ring/Element diamond, steel-grey shield/Protection crest, cyan deflection arc/Parry crest. No holes or trypophobic patterns, honeycombs, perforations, vents, porous textures, rivet clusters, gems, crystals, runes, fantasy filigree, text or numbers. All icons contained within individual square cells with even transparent padding and separated lighting.

Built-in image_gen verwendet; kein CLI-/API-Fallback. Die Formulierung oben hält die vollständige Gestaltungsabsicht des verwendeten Prompts fest.
