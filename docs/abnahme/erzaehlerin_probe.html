Erzählerin: Hörprobe (5 Ortsbilder)

Stimme „Erzaehlerin“, Chatterbox Turbo. Nur Probe, nicht im Spiel.

1 · Something That Blinks

You're walking back to the tender when something stops you. On the stone wall above the quay stands a goat, perfectly calm, as if the day had nothing to do with her. Around her neck is a collar, and on the collar a small red light is blinking, slowly, like a heartbeat. Then she turns and climbs the wall, and the macchia swallows her whole. For a moment Casanova's eyes follow her up to the dark line of the mountains.

2 · The Rock Niche

The niche is deeper than it looked. A boar has made its bed in front of it, and an ape is crouching on the ledge above.

3 · The Air Pocket

A dome of rock with air in it, and a shelf just above the high-water mark. Matteu pulls himself up next to you, shivering. Then he holds the lamp to the wall and stops shivering.

4 · Tracks and Nothing Else

For three days Bibi has found nothing but prints, on walls, on roofs, and on your boat once. Now she's found a dead goat high on a rock, and she has gone pale. You look up. There's nothing there. You look up again.

5 · The Third Step

The water is lapping at the third step. Totò is standing up in the dinghy, waving both arms. When he sees Gennaro, he stops waving and just stands there.
