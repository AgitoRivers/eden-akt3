The night is warm, and the bay barely moves. One light still burns in the workshop. Rosi has rolled up her sleeves, and there is oil on her collarbone she hasn't noticed. Or maybe she has. She looks up when you come in, and she takes her time looking back down. The door is still open behind you. You could close it. Nobody on this boat would hear. Day nine is not over yet.

The bay is quiet tonight. The Eden Rise lies at anchor, and one light burns in the workshop. Rosi is there, with oil on her hands and a photo she thinks nobody has seen. Down on the quay, Lola is looking for fish sauce again, and Bà Tư is pretending not to know her. You could go to bed. But the generator is getting louder, and nobody else has noticed. Day nine is not over yet.
