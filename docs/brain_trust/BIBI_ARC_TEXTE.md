Bibi benennt alles, auch Duc. Sie ist in Frankreich aufgewachsen, ihre Mutter ist Deutsche. Beide Sprachen nennen Geliebte gern nach Tieren. Sie erklärt ein Wort höchstens einmal, und nie zwei in derselben Szene.

**Technik:** Im Figurenrahmen drei Anreden je Stufe, weil jede Anrede eigene Aufnahmen braucht. Alle anderen fest in Szenen und Selfies, dem Modell als Liste für freie Gespräche.

## 2. Vor dem ersten Treffen: die Näpfe

Drei Funde in der Macchia vor der Quest, jeweils ein Blechnapf mit Zettel:

- *“Eat this. Not the hikers. – B.”*
- *“If you can read this, you are not a wolf. Please put the bowl back. – B.”*
- *“This one is for the hyenas. Everybody else, I can see you. – B.”*

Nonna Fiora, einmal am Kloster, ohne Namen:

- *“A girl leaves bowls on my hill. I put soup in them when she is gone. She thinks the animals are doing very well.”*

## 3. Das Versprechen am Feuer (`rt_lager`)

Neu nach Charlys Satz „That's the whole job“, Duc hört versteckt zu:

- **Bibi:** “You two can run if you have to. I'm staying until every one of them has that thing off its neck. I owe them that. And when Julien comes looking for them, I want to be the first thing he sees.” (pride)
- **Bibi:** “With grenades! Ha ha! ...Sorry. I do that.” (nervousness)

## 4. Die Stations-Idee (`rt_wn_bibi`)

Neu vor *“I could go.”*:

- **Bibi:** “I said I'd stay, you know. At the fire. That was when I still thought I was one of the good ones.” (sadness)

Antwort Ja, ersetzt „Why don't you look after them?“:

- Chip: Looks up at the macchia. “You said until every collar was off. I heard you at the fire.”
- **Bibi:** “You were listening? From behind a tree? That's really creepy, Clipboard.” (surprise)
- danach wie bisher: “Me? With what? I haven't got a fence, I haven't got any feed, I've got – I've got a bowl.” / “...I've got a bowl.”

Antwort Nein, neu vor der Giraffe:

- **Bibi:** “I said I'd stay. I say a lot of things.” (sadness)

## 5. Gemeinsam arbeiten: fünf Events

Kurze wholesome Vertrauens-Events an der Station, je eine Phase, mit Chips nach Rahmen-Muster. Der Name kommt mit der Stufe.

**The Fence (ab `mochis_offen`):**
- “Hold this post. No, straight. No, your other straight. Have you ever held anything, Clipboard?”
- “That's the worst fence I've ever seen. Good. Monday can't work out how to get through it either, because neither of us knows where it starts.”

**Half a Roof (Vertrauen 2):**
- “Pass me the nails, mon lapin. Not all of them. Why would I need all of them?”
- “We have now got three quarters of a roof. I'm telling everybody. I'm having it printed on something.”

**Bath Day (Vertrauen 2):**
- “Truffle doesn't like water, so you hold the front and I'll hold the back. The front is the bad end. I should have said that first.”
- “You've got mud in your ear, mon canard. No, leave it. It suits you.”

**The Bottle (Vertrauen 3, Nacht):**
- “Pudding won't take it from me tonight, so you try. Sit down, go slow, and don't look at it like a report.”
- “Look at that. It's asleep on your arm. So now you can't move for an hour, Bärchen. I don't make the rules.”

**The Spanner (Vertrauen 4):**
- “Petru wants his spanner back, and Monsieur wants to keep it, and I've told them both that it's a matter for the courts.”
- “You came up here on your day off to help me steal from an ape, ma puce. I just want you to know that I noticed.”

## 6. Ökotourismus: *A Very Honest Refund*

Nach *The Spanner*, abends auf der Mauer. Setzt `mo_eco_idee`.

- **Bibi:** “Rich people keep asking if they can come up and see the animals. They want a photo with the wolf, and the answer's no. But I've been thinking. What if they pay?”
- **Bibi:** “Forty euros, to walk up a hill with one of us and maybe see nothing. No cages and no feeding show. If the snow leopard doesn't feel like it, you get a very nice walk.”
- **Bibi:** “There's no refund. I'd like that written very clearly. You do numbers, mon chou. Is that the stupidest thing you've ever heard?”

Chips:
- *vertrauen:* “No. It's what I've been telling companies for ten years, and nobody said it that well.”
- *begehren:* “It's a terrible business plan. Show me the rest of it.”
- *beide:* “Fifty euros. People trust it more if it hurts.”

Woche 4, nach Buenaventuras Schwur, ein Satz zu Duc:

- **Bibi:** “So he's got forty men, a boat and nothing to do? And they know every rock in that bay? Doudou. Doudou, come here. I need you to do some numbers.”

## 7. Das Geheimnis: *The Brochure*

Vertrauen 4, nach `mo_eco_idee`. Bei Elsas Unterlagen liegt ein ARK-Prospekt, Duc liest ihn an der Station.

Lage: You read the brochure out loud, because you read this kind of thing for a living. *“Ark. A rescue programme for animals with nowhere else to go.”*

- Chip (fest): “Whoever wrote this was good. That's the best greenwashing I've seen in years.”
- **Bibi:** “Thank you.” (sadness)
- **Bibi:** “I was twenty-two. I had a notebook with a zoo in it, no fences, and I'd been drawing it since school. I showed it to Julien on our third date. He said it was beautiful, and he asked if he could keep it.” (sadness)
- **Bibi:** “He kept it. That's my sentence. I told you everything, you know, every animal and every name. I didn't tell you that one.” (embarrassment)

Chips:
- *vertrauen:* “You wrote it because you meant it. He's the one who sold it.”
- *begehren:* “Show me the notebook. The real one.”
- *beide:* “Then you're the best copywriter Alon ever had. Let's take it back.”

Folge: Der Bogen zur Kommission und gegen Vasseur kann das aufgreifen.

## 9. Das Geständnis: Ducs Seite im Field Book

Ab Stufe 4 erscheint unter den schwarzen Tier-Umrissen einer, der wie ein Mensch aussieht. Niemand kommentiert ihn. Nach den Toren im Epilog gibt sie Duc das Buch:

- **Bibi:** “That's all of them, every page. Well, nearly. Have you looked at the last one? You don't have to look at the last one. Did you know a vulture can digest bones? So that's interesting.” (nervousness)

Seite mit Foto von Duc, darauf der Schlamm aus *Bath Day*:

> *Name:* ~~Clipboard~~ ~~Mon lapin~~ ~~Hase~~ ~~Mon canard~~ ~~Mon chou~~ ~~Bärchen~~ Spatz
> *Favourite food:* mine, sadly
> *Bites:* only people he likes
> *Release:* no. ...Ask him first.

- **Bibi:** “I stayed in Marseille for a man once, and this is not that. This place is mine, and it stays mine. And I'm not signing for you. I signed for things before. I'm only asking.” (nervousness)

Chips:
- *vertrauen:* “Ask me, then.”
- *begehren:* “You've spelled it wrong. It's only got one bite.”
- *beide:* “I saw the outline weeks ago. I've been trying to walk like it.”

Danach noch eine Zeile, leise, ohne Witz:

- **Bibi:** “Spatz. My mother called me that. Now you know everything. This time it's true.” (gratitude)
